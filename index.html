Section 1

Stop Tracking These Metrics

These metrics make you feel productive, but they do not tell you if you are making money.

F

Followers

A bigger audience does not matter if the right people never raise their hand.

L

Likes

Likes can mean agreement, entertainment, or habit. They do not prove buying intent.

V

Views

Views show reach. They do not show whether your content moved anyone closer to becoming a client.

Section 2

The Content Conversion Stack

The only four metrics that show whether your content is moving people from attention to buying interest.

1

Saves

Signal: Value

If people are not saving your content, it is not valuable enough.

  • Add frameworks
  • Add step-by-step breakdowns
  • Add come-back-to-this content
2

Shares

Signal: Emotional impact

If people are not sharing, your content is not hitting hard enough.

  • Use stronger opinions
  • Use callouts like "you are behind"
  • Name relatable pain
3

Comments

Signal: Intent

Comments are people raising their hand.

  • Use a clear keyword CTA
  • Use polarizing statements
  • Ask questions that force a response
4

Profile Clicks

Signal: Buying interest

This is the closest metric to money before a DM or booked call.

  • Write a stronger hook
  • Create a curiosity gap
  • Make your positioning obvious

Section 4

What To Do Next

One specific move for each bottleneck, so the audit turns into your next post.

Low saves

Make your next post a step-by-step system people want to reference later.

Low comments

End with a keyword CTA, like "comment SYSTEM and I will send you the framework."

Low clicks

Rewrite the hook to create curiosity and make your positioning impossible to miss.

No DMs

Tighten the offer so the reader knows exactly what problem you solve and what to do next.
