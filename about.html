About

Coaching for developers who want a clearer path into software engineering.

I help early-career developers turn scattered effort into a focused job search, stronger technical preparation, and a portfolio that hiring teams can understand.

Your Story

From learning to launching.

Many Computer Science graduates and junior engineers have the ability to do the work, but their resumes, projects, interview habits, and search strategy do not yet communicate that ability. Coaching closes that gap with direct feedback and a practical plan.

The work is built for people who have already started programming and now need a professional system for getting noticed, preparing well, and staying consistent.

Mission

Help capable developers become hireable candidates.

The goal is simple: give early-career software engineers the structure, feedback, and accountability they need to land roles that match their ambition.

Why I Coach

Because talent needs direction, not vague advice.

Practical experience helping developers

Coaching sessions focus on the real materials and moments that decide whether a candidate moves forward: resumes, LinkedIn, projects, interviews, and outreach.

Focused feedback every week

You get specific next steps instead of generic motivation, then return with work to review and improve.

A job search built around execution

The process turns goals into weekly actions so you know what to build, apply to, practice, and refine.
